# Damage resolution: shields first, armor on spillover

Damage resolves as: shields absorb up to raw_damage fully, then armor reduces only the spillover (damage past shields). The alternative — armor applies to raw damage first, then shields absorb the remainder — would make armor universally effective regardless of shield state. Chosen order rewards shield-stacking against burst damage and makes armor matter most when shields are depleted, creating meaningful differentiation between shield-heavy and armor-heavy builds.
